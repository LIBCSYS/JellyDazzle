# P01 Chasm

## Look
Neon aurora over a deep indigo chasm: cyan and cobalt sink into near-black violet, then climb back out through orchid, magenta, peach and mint to electric accents (5efdf7/ff5dcc/fdfe89).

## Mood
Mood: subterranean bioluminescence — cool, deep, glowing. The dark 32313b core makes every bright hue pop without strobing.

## Pattern families
Best for: tunnel/zoom patterns, plasma interference, radial kaleidoscopes where the dark core sits at center and neon rims flow outward. The cyclic ramp is naturally smooth (blue->violet->magenta->warm->mint->cyan) so it suits continuous-phase patterns (sine plasma, moire) with zero reordering.
