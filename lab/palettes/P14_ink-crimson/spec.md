# P14 Ink Crimson

## Look
Crimson noir: scarlet through blood red and wine into violet-black ink, with a three-step ice counterstrike (0ce6f2 cyan, 0098db azure, 1e579c steel blue) at the deep end.

## Mood
Mood: the drama card — monochrome-red menace with one cold gleam; maximum mood shift from the candy/neon sets.

## Pattern families
Best for: pulse/heartbeat radial patterns, slow rotating blades, ink-drop marbling. Use the cyan accent sparingly as the moving element against the red field — one bright mote circling a dark crimson kaleidoscope is hypnotic.
