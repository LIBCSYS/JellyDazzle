# P11 Azure Abyss

## Look
A nine-step descent: pale seafoam and aqua down through azure and cobalt into abyssal navy-black.

## Mood
Mood: ocean depth — single-family blue, serene and vast; the most restful palette in the set.

## Pattern families
Best for: caustic light patterns, slow sine-wave interference, breathing radial gradients, jellyfish/medusa kaleidoscopes. Monotonic value makes it a drop-in luminance ramp; pair its darkest tone with any one accent color from another palette for an instant two-tone scheme.
