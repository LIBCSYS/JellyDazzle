# P07 Bubblegum 16

## Look
Arcade candy: black licorice ground, cherry red, orange, lemon, white, then bubblegum pinks, grape purples, blueberry blues and a lime accent.

## Mood
Mood: jukebox playful — sugar-bright primaries kept honest by the black anchor; cheerful without pastel wash-out.

## Pattern families
Best for: bold tiled patterns, bouncing-shape mosaics, stepped concentric rings, pinwheel kaleidoscopes. Treat as flat fills over the 1a1c2c dark; the palette's high internal contrast keeps small kaleidoscope cells readable at 320x240.
