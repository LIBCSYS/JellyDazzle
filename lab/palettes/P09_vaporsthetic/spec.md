# P09 Vaporsthetic

## Look
Miami dusk: rose, salmon and cream sands against seafoam, teal and mint waters, grounded by plum-mauve shadows.

## Mood
Mood: vaporwave calm — the softest set in the collection that still has full value range; nostalgic, warm-cool balanced.

## Pattern families
Best for: slow horizontal-wave patterns, sunset-grid landscapes, gentle moire and ripple kaleidoscopes. Use when the app should breathe between intense neon schemes — same kaleidoscope math, gentler emotional register.
