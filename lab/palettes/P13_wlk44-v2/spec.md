# P13 WLK44 v2

## Look
The master spectrum: 44 colors covering full-value ramps of red, orange, gold, green, teal, blue, violet and magenta plus clean neutrals.

## Mood
Mood: universal donor — a complete, professionally balanced gamut; whatever hue the math lands on, a good version of it exists here.

## Pattern families
Best for: patterns that need arbitrary hue rotation (hue-cycling plasma, rainbow spirals, phase-mapped fractals). Also the fallback quantization target: any RGB frame remapped to wlk44-v2 instantly looks like it belongs in the app.
