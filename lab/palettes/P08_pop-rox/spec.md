# P08 Pop Rox

## Look
Candy jewels: coral-red through salmon and cream on the warm side, midnight navy through violet, magenta and rose on the cool side.

## Mood
Mood: pop-art gemstone — juicy warm centers wrapped in cool velvet darks; rich and slightly luxurious.

## Pattern families
Best for: gem/facet kaleidoscopes, nested polygon spinners, quilted diamond tilings. Map pattern phase to the full cycle: the warm arc and cool arc alternate as the phase rotates, which gives that 'never the same color scheme' shimmer as the cycle drifts.
