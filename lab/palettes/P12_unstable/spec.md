# P12 Unstable

## Look
A 32-color jewel riot in ordered ramps: violet->grape, cobalt->sky, jade->lime, wine->scarlet->amber->gold, magenta->blush, rose->salmon, teal->ice, slate->white.

## Mood
Mood: kaleidoscope incarnate — eight short gem ramps, all saturated, all sharing deep anchors; maximal variety that still coheres.

## Pattern families
Best for: multi-arm kaleidoscopes where each arm indexes a different sub-ramp, patchwork tilings, rotating segment wheels. Indexing sub-ramps by segment number recreates the original dazzle trick of 'every region its own scheme, all belonging together'.
