# P03 Technogarten

## Look
An electric garden: violet and periwinkle beds, blue-to-teal-to-green vines, with rose and blush blooms and a black void row.

## Mood
Mood: synthetic organic — cool neon foliage with warm floral accents; lush, not harsh.

## Pattern families
Best for: organic flow fields, vine-like curl noise, petal/rosette kaleidoscopes, reaction-diffusion looks. Split use: greens+blues for the field, pinks reserved for moving highlights so accents read as blossoms drifting through the pattern.
