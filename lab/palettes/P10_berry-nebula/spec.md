# P10 Berry Nebula

## Look
Eight-step space ramp: bright cyan sinking through teal into blackberry darkness, then rising through royal purple and orchid to hot pink.

## Mood
Mood: deep-space berry — cosmic, mysterious, with a perfect V-shaped value curve (bright ends, dark middle) that reads as glowing gas around a void.

## Pattern families
Best for: starfield/nebula clouds, radial vortex kaleidoscopes with the dark tone at the event-horizon center, orbit-trap fractals. As a cyclic ramp it is seamless: cyan->dark->pink->(wrap)->cyan flows without a hard seam.
