# P06 Fireball 32

## Look
Fire and its afterimage: white-hot core through yellow, orange, crimson and ember browns, answered by a cool half of cobalt, sky blue, mint green and violet-purple.

## Mood
Mood: elemental duel — heat against cold, both saturated, hinged on shared dark maroons and indigos.

## Pattern families
Best for: two-field patterns (fire field vs plasma field), yin-yang kaleidoscope arms, comet/particle trails (warm heads, cool tails). The 32-color spread also survives being split into two independent 16-color cyclic ramps.
