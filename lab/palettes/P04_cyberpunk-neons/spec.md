# P04 Cyberpunk Neons

## Look
Teal-to-cyan ramp on near-black navy, opposed by a crimson-to-rose ramp: two enemy gradients sharing one dark city sky.

## Mood
Mood: neo-noir signage — rain-slick streets lit by rival neon signs; dramatic, high contrast, cohesive because both ramps share the same dark base.

## Pattern families
Best for: dual-armed kaleidoscopes (one arm teal, one crimson), interference of two plasma fields each mapped to one ramp, scanline/grid patterns. Keep the two ramps spatially interleaved but temporally slow — the contrast does the work.
