# P15 Holographic Mode

## Look
Eight iridescent pastels: pearl white through mint, sky, periwinkle and lilac — a soap-bubble sheen ramp.

## Mood
Mood: holo-foil serenity — bright, cool, weightless; the palate cleanser between saturated schemes.

## Pattern families
Best for: interference-fringe patterns, thin-film swirls, slow conic gradients, soft-edged kaleidoscope petals. Low internal contrast means it needs large smooth shapes (no fine detail); ideal for the gentlest motion states of the app.
