# P02 Funky Future 8

## Look
Eight bold retrowave tones: deep violet ground, hot magenta and pink, cream highlight, orange and banana yellow, cobalt and sky cyan.

## Mood
Mood: 80s arcade confidence — loud but curated; every pair of colors contrasts hard yet belongs together.

## Pattern families
Best for: hard-edged geometry — spinning polygons, dazzle stripes, chevron kaleidoscopes, tiled mosaics. With only 8 colors, use as flat fills with dithered boundaries or as a coarse cyclic ramp for stepped plasma bands. The violet 2b0f54 is the anchor background.
