# P05 Sunraze

## Look
A pure dusk ramp: near-black plum through wine, crimson, vermilion, orange, gold, peach, to pale cream.

## Mood
Mood: slow sunset burn — warm, monotonic, meditative; the single-hue-family sweep reads as light itself, never garish.

## Pattern families
Best for: any continuous-phase pattern — plasma, radial waves, height-mapped ripples — where the ramp maps directly to intensity. Because it is monotonic in value, it doubles as a luminance palette for metaballs and soft radial glows. Cyclic wrap (cream->plum) gives a satisfying slow 'day rolls into night' loop.
