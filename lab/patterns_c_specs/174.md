# 174 — Braid Loom

Five concentric torus braids that are genuinely over and under, not merely overlapping.
Each ring carries m = 3-5 cords whose radius wobbles as `R + A sin(f*theta + 2*pi*j/m)`
with **f = P + 1/m**, P an integer: the extra 1/m means that after one lap every cord has
slid into its neighbour's starting slot, so the braid closes on itself the way a real
plait does rather than each strand closing on itself. The cosine of the same argument is
the cord's depth. The ring is then painted in theta order, and at every step the cords are
insertion-sorted back-to-front; each one first clears a casing and then lays down its
core, so a cord passing behind is physically cut out of the canvas by the cord in front.
The casing is a thin **slab, not a disc**, and it is oriented along the cord's true
direction of travel `dP/dtheta = (r' cos - r sin, r' sin + r cos)` — the circle's tangent
is wrong by up to 19 degrees where the wobble is steep, and using it notches the cord
periodically and turns every rope into a dotted line. Cores are drawn with a
`q(0.45+0.55q)` falloff under a max blend, which gives each cord a lit crown and a shaded
rim so it reads as a tube. Rings counter-rotate at different rates over a slow global
breath. 75% near-black, mean frame delta 0.2 — the quietest routine in the set.
