# 151 Chladni Filaments

**Look.** A black plate crossed by thin, luminous curves that bend and re-knot like sand
on a vibrating drumhead — closed loops, X-crossings and long diagonals that stretch,
merge and split without ever cutting. Colour runs as a diagonal two-stop wash across the
figure (a narrow, restrained band on some seeds, a wide one on others) with a white-hot
core where the lines are brightest; better than 80% of the frame stays at zero, so it is
an overlay layer, not a ground.

**Math.** Superposition of six square-plate eigenmodes in Chladni's antisymmetric form,
`f(x,y) = SUM_k a_k [cos(n_k pi x) cos(m_k pi y) - cos(m_k pi x) cos(n_k pi y)]`, with
`n_k, m_k` drawn per segment from 1..7. The image is the *zero set* of `f`, lit with a
Lorentzian ridge `1/(1 + (g f)^2)` (g ~ 11-14) so only the nodal lines glow and the
antinodes stay black. Motion comes from two places and neither can jump: the amplitudes
`a_k = sin(w_k t)|sin(w_k t)|` breathe on six incommensurate slow sines (the `x|x|`
shaping makes each mode dwell near zero rather than snap through it), and a global plate
scale `s(t)` drifts +-15%, which is still separable, so the whole field is rebuilt each
frame from two cosine tables (one per axis, one row per distinct order) and a 12-term dot
product per pixel — 480x360 canvas, bilinear upscale to the framebuffer. Measured
313 fps at 1280x960, frame-to-frame delta 0.24-1.26.
