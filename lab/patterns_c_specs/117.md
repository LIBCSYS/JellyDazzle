# 117 — Cloth Lattice

A wireframe sheet the size of the frame, rolling in slow standing waves — a lit-from-nowhere
grid of wires whose hue follows height and whose brightness follows depth, seen in
three-quarter perspective. The shape is never scripted: a 34x26 grid runs the discrete wave
equation `u'' = c^2 laplacian(u)` with c^2 = 0.028, the border pinned and damping 0.9994, and
three driver points on slow orbits breathe energy in at 0.0125-0.019 rad/frame. Three
stabilisers make it screensaver-safe, and each was added because the harness caught the
failure it fixes: the velocity field is spatially smoothed each step (viscosity — kills the
grid-scale modes that shimmer), an automatic gain control eases the whole field toward a fixed
RMS at a quarter percent per frame with the per-frame gain clamped to +/-0.25% (a coherent
driver would otherwise pump a resonance without bound, and a fast gain change moves every wire
at once), and a tanh limiter caps any single node so a driver parked in one spot cannot dig a
well deeper than the global relief. Nodes are yawed, pitched and perspective-projected; edges
are stroked with depth-cued brightness. The seed drives colour only — the membrane state is
persistent, so a segment change never cuts the shape. 214 fps, mean delta 0.83, worst 6.2.
