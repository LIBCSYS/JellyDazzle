# 144 — Newton Filigree

**Look.** Tri-tone lace on pure black: chains of beaded loops strung between six-armed
starbursts, each thread trimmed in a different third of the palette. It drifts, rotates
and breathes; loops open into spirals, spirals braid, braids close.

**Math.** Newton's method on `z^3 = c`, over-relaxed:
`z <- z - a (z^3 - c) / (3 z^2)`, eight iterations from every pixel of a 320x240 grid.
Interiors converge quadratically and are left black; only the fractal basin boundary still
has residual `|z^3 - c|` after eight steps, and that residual, tone-mapped
`1-exp(-26 r)`, is what is lit. Two parameters drive the family: the relaxation factor
`a = 1.10 + 0.24 sin(0.00047 t)` (over-relaxation is what turns plain basins into beaded
spirals) and `c = e^{i 0.00091 t}` walking the unit circle, which rotates the three roots
and drags the whole filigree with it. The nearest of the three roots gives each pixel its
palette third. View rotation, a breathing 3.05 ± 0.75 zoom and a slow pan complete the
motion. The intensity field is eased 3:1 against the previous frame, which removes all
boundary shimmer and holds the measured delta at 0.28.
