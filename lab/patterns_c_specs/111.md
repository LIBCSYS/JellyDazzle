# 111 — Physarum Veins

A living network of glowing veins that keeps rebuilding itself: thick trunk routes brighten
and thicken, starved side branches dim and dissolve, junctions migrate, loops close and
reopen. Nothing about the shape is drawn — it is what 42000 agents and a chemical field
negotiate. Each agent samples the chemo-attractant field at three points, 8.5 px ahead and
+/-0.40 rad to either side, turns 0.72 rad toward whichever sample is strongest (plus a
0.045 rad jitter), steps 0.95 px and deposits its colour where it lands; the field is then
diffused with a separable 1-2-1 kernel and decayed by 0.93. That deposit-then-follow loop is
the whole model (Jones 2010), and the positive feedback between the two is what condenses a
uniform swarm into transport veins. Sensor distance, sensor angle and turn rate breathe on
slow incommensurate sines (periods 100-170 s), which walks the network between a fine mesh
and a few fat highways. Deposit hue is `hbase + 0.21x + 0.15y + 9*heading`, position
dominating, so a vein keeps one colour along its length instead of averaging to white; the
field itself is the image, tone-mapped `c/(0.55+c)` and bilinear-upscaled from 640x480.
82-91% near-black, so it composites as a glow overlay. 162 fps, mean delta 0.51.
