# 176 — Bob Rosette

Amiga vector bobs strung on a 3-D Lissajous knot. Ninety soft balls are spaced along
`(sin(a u), sin(b u + p), sin(c u + q))` and slide along it forever at 0.0016 rad/frame,
so the necklace flows through a fixed shape rather than the shape thrashing. Each ball is
a `(1 - d^2/r^2)^2` additive splat, sized 5-16 px and brightened by its own depth, drawn
over a hairline thread that follows the same curve — bead and wire together read as a
turning 3-D object without a single shading calculation. The frequency triple morphs
between five presets drawn from a table of eight integer triples every 640 frames. The one
non-obvious decision: the morph interpolates the **vertices of two closed curves**, not
the frequencies. Lerping frequencies passes through non-integer values, and a Lissajous
with a non-integer frequency ratio does not close — the necklace tears open and the thread
whips a straight line across the frame for four seconds. Interpolating positions between
two closed knots keeps the figure closed at every instant of the crossfade. Scale is set
so that the corner of the unit box plus the largest bob radius still clears the frame
edge under maximum perspective. 88% near-black, mean frame delta 0.5.
