# 127 — Dipole Filings

Iron filings around three drifting magnets. Three 2-D magnetic dipoles orbit the
centre on an ellipse while their moment vectors rotate at their own rates and their
strengths breathe; the superposed field is
`B = sum_k [ 2 (m_k . r_k) r_k / |r_k|^2 - m_k ] / |r_k|^2`. Field lines are that
field integrated: 22 seed angles at each of four seed radii (5.5, 12, 26, 55 px)
around each magnet, walked up to 340 unit steps, alternate seeds walking the field
backwards so both halves of every loop get drawn. A line terminates when it re-enters
a magnet or leaves the frame. Because the magnets move slowly and B is a smooth
function of their positions, the entire filing pattern flows rather than reshuffling —
the only discontinuities are individual lines crossing a separatrix near a neutral
point, which is one strand in 264 and reads as the pattern re-routing, which is what
real filings do. Hue advances with arclength so each strand runs outward through the
palette from its source, and weight falls from 430 to 100 over the walk so the near
field is bright and the far arcs are ghosts. 264 lines, ~60k additive bilinear splats
per frame, ~500 fps. The sparsest routine in the set after the knot:
92% near-black, ideal for SCREEN compositing.
