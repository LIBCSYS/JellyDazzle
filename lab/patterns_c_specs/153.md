# 153 Loxodrome Drift

**Look.** Two still points on a black field with a whole head of luminous hair combed
between them: every filament spirals out of the source, sweeps across the frame and
winds infinitely tight into the sink. Pulses of light run along the filaments toward the
sink, the two poles drift slowly around each other, and the whole comb precesses. Hue
runs from one pole's colour to the other's along each strand. Very sparse — black
between every filament.

**Math.** A loxodromic Moebius transformation with fixed points p and q is conjugate to
multiplication by `lambda = e^(a + i theta)`; in the log coordinate
`w = log((z-p)/(z-q))` it is a pure translation, so its orbits are straight lines in a
cylinder (period 2pi in Im w) and double spirals on screen. The routine draws that
family directly: streamlines are parallel lines in w at direction `d = (a, theta)/|.|`,
enumerated by perpendicular offset over one period `2 pi a / |log lambda|`, and each is
walked in `Re w` from -3.7 to +3.7 — walking the log coordinate rather than arc length
is what keeps every filament spanning pole-to-pole no matter how tight the pitch. Each
sample is mapped back with `z = (p - q e^w)/(1 - e^w)` and drawn as a splatted segment,
brightness `exp(-0.135 u^2)` (fade into the poles) times a travelling pulse
`sin(2.3 u - 0.021 t)`. 480x360 canvas, one blur, exponential tone map, upscale.
Measured 175-305 fps at 1280x960, delta 1.30-2.24.
