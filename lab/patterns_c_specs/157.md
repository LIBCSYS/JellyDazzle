# 157 Supershape Bloom

**Look.** Twelve nested outlines on black, each a Gielis supershape: starfish, gear
wheels, pinched pentagrams, rounded polygons and soft blobs, all breathing between those
states at their own pace, each ring turning at its own slow rate and drifting through the
palette from the inner ring to the outer. A sparse rosette stack — line art with the
whole interior at zero.

**Math.** The superformula
`r(th) = (|cos(m th/4)/a|^n2 + |sin(m th/4)/b|^n3)^(-1/n1)` with a = b = 1. Each ring
holds a fixed integer symmetry `m` (two values per segment, alternating ring to ring, so
the stack stays coherent) while `n1, n2, n3` breathe on three incommensurate slow sines
per ring — that is the whole morph, and because r is continuous in the exponents the
shape can never jump. Each ring is sampled at 600 angles into a scratch radius buffer,
normalised by its own peak (so a ring that grows spikes does not blow past its
neighbours), scaled to its shell radius, rotated by `t * spin_j`, and drawn as splatted
segments with a brightness wave rolling outward through the stack. 480x360 canvas, one
blur, exponential tone map, upscale. Measured 241-300 fps at 1280x960, delta 0.28-0.53.
