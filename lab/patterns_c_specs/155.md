# 155 Knot Loom

**Look.** A three-dimensional torus knot hanging in black space as glowing wire: three
staggered strands braiding through each other, the near passes bright and warm, the far
passes dim and cool, tumbling on two axes. Every twenty seconds or so the knot melts
into a different knot — a trefoil relaxing into a cinquefoil — without a cut. The
sparsest routine in the set; almost all of the frame is zero.

**Math.** Standard torus-knot parametrisation
`x = (1 + r cos q phi) cos p phi`, `y = (1 + r cos q phi) sin p phi`, `z = r sin q phi`,
evaluated on the same 900-point phi grid for two different `(p,q)` pairs drawn per
segment and linearly interpolated by `alpha(t)`, a cosine passed twice through
smoothstep so it dwells on each knot and crosses quickly but continuously; because both
endpoints of the blend are closed curves, every intermediate curve is closed too, so no
seam can appear. Three strands use staggered tube radii (0.34, 0.47, 0.60) and phase
offsets. Points are rotated about X and Y at 0.0031 and 0.0019 rad/frame, perspective
projected (`k = d/(d - z)`, d = 6.4), and consecutive samples joined by additive splatted
segments with weight `(0.13 + 1.30 dep^3) k`, `dep` being normalised depth — which is
what makes the braid read as solid rather than flat. Hue is indexed by depth. 480x360
canvas, blur, exponential tone map, upscale. Measured 265-306 fps, delta 1.01-3.10.
