# 101 — Physarum Veil

A living vein network, drawn by 7000 agents that can only follow their own slime.
Each agent samples a diffusing trail map at three points ahead — left, centre and
right of its heading at 9 px and ±0.52 rad — turns 0.36 rad toward the strongest
reading, steps one pixel and deposits attractant; the map is then convolved with a
3×3 box and multiplied by 0.905. That is the whole rule set, and it is enough to
reproduce what *Physarum polycephalum* does in a petri dish: a uniform haze
condenses into travelling fronts, the fronts anneal into veins, the veins fatten
into trunk routes with fine tributaries, and closed cells open and close as the
colony keeps re-routing. Left alone the network coarsens until only two or three
trunks survive, so a rolling 34 agents per frame are re-scattered — the whole
population turns over about every 4.5 s — which pins the mesh at a steady scale and
keeps it reorganising forever. Trail concentration drives both brightness (an
exponential tone curve) and hue, so trunks, tributaries and exploratory haze read as
three different colour families of the same scheme. 320×240 simulation, bilinear
upscale. Overlay-qualified: ~71% near-black, and what is lit is filament, not field.
