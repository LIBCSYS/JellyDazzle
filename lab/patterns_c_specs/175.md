# 175 — Glenz Vectors

The Amiga transparent-polygon trick, two solids deep. A cube and an icosahedron are drawn
interpenetrating with every one of their 26 faces filled additively and **nothing culled**
— no backface rejection, no z-buffer, no sorting — so the picture is literally the sum of
all faces: where two overlap the colour doubles, where four overlap it blazes, and the
solids read as blown glass whose internal structure is visible. That summation is the
whole effect, and it is also why the routine needs no depth machinery at all. Faces are
scan-converted through a per-row min/max span buffer with a generation stamp (so a row an
edge never touched can never be filled with a stale span) and with fractional coverage at
both ends of every span, which keeps the silhouettes soft enough to survive the upscale;
each face's outline is then laid over its own fill a little brighter, the classic glenz
highlight. Face weight rises with the face centroid's depth so the near faces dominate.
The two solids counter-rotate on different Euler triples at 0.0018-0.0044 rad/frame and
the icosahedron breathes 5% against the cube. 67% near-black — the densest layer in the
set, and the one that works best as ground rather than overlay. Mean frame delta 0.6.
