# 171 — Physarum Veil

A slime-mould colony writing its own transport network, live. 22,000 agents each carry a
position and a unit heading; every frame each one samples the pheromone field at three
points 3-7 px ahead (ahead-left, ahead, ahead-right), turns toward the strongest, and
steps 0.45 px. Wherever it lands it deposits, and the field is then blurred with a 5-tap
kernel and multiplied by a decay of 0.905-0.95. That is the whole Jones physarum model,
and it is enough: the plate first fogs over, then the fog condenses into veins, then the
veins prune each other into a lace of bright cords with a fine capillary mesh strung
between them. Two departures from the textbook. First, the heading is stored as a
**vector**, not an angle, and the sensor and turn rotations are applied as 2x2 rotations
by precomputed sines — no `sinf`/`cosf` anywhere in the 22k-agent loop, which is what buys
the frame budget. Second, 70 agents per frame are re-scattered to random positions with
random headings; without that trickle of scouts the colony coarsens into three fat cords
by frame 1200 and stops being a network at all. Colour comes from a 64-entry ramp indexed
by `1 - exp(-v)` of the local pheromone, with a quartic lift toward white so only the very
hottest cord centres go pale — the restrained, near-monochrome end of the palette
taxonomy. Field resets at `sl == 0`. 80% near-black, mean frame delta 0.55.
