# 106 — Medusa Drift

A bloom of jellyfish, lit from inside. Seven medusae rise through dark water on jet
propulsion: the bell contracts over about a second and relaxes over two (a cosine
skewed by `cos(φ + 0.55 sin φ)`), and the animal only accelerates while it is
squeezing, so the rise is integrated from the pulse rather than animated separately —
the characteristic lurch-and-glide of a real swimming medusa. The bell is an ellipse
field sampled over its bounding box, with a tight bright rim at the ellipse boundary,
a wide soft halo outside it and a translucent interior, and it is faded out below the
margin so the bell reads as an open dome rather than a disc; five radial canals are
drawn inside it. Nine tentacles hang from each margin as sine filaments whose phase
lags the bell by a quarter period and whose amplitude grows down their length, so
they crack and stream a beat behind every squeeze. Bell aspect swings from
1.30:0.62 to 0.90:1.04 across the pulse. Hue is per animal from the live palette with
the oral arms 26 steps off the bell. ~93% near-black and the animals are small: a
glow overlay that leaves the ground visible between them.
