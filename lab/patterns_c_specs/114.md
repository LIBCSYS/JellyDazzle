# 114 — Khatim Weave

Interlaced eight-fold Islamic strapwork: octagonal bands of two colours, each with a black
edge, passing alternately over and under one another across the whole frame, with the khatim
star-and-cross figure cut where the two families cross. Two square lattices, one offset half
a cell from the other, each carry an octagon outline; the octagonal metric
`d = max(|u|, |v|, (|u|+|v|)/sqrt2)` gives the outline exactly with no polygon list, and the
strap is `|d - r| < w` tested twice, once for a bright core and once for a wider black edge.
Which family passes over is decided by the parity of the cell index `(cx + cy) & 1`, so the
weave is a genuine interlace rather than an overlap; because the "under" band's black edge
survives at the crossing, the illusion of depth comes for free. The cell fold is carried
incrementally along each scanline — no floor, no float-to-int conversion in the inner loop —
and the light running along each strap is a 1024-entry sine table indexed by the along-band
coordinate. The panel rotates about a degree every fifteen seconds and breathes 8% in cell
size. Reads as line art on near-black (67-83%) but dense enough to serve as a lattice ground.
127 fps, mean delta 1.24.
