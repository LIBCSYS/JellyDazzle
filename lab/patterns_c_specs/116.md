# 116 — Hilbert Unfold

A single continuous ribbon caught mid-construction: it collapses toward a fat order-2 zigzag,
grows its order-5 detail back one scale at a time, ripples, and unfolds again, never crossing
itself and never cutting. The order-6 Hilbert curve (4096 cells) is precomputed once by the
standard bit-rotation `d2xy`, and so is the position of every one of its points at each
coarser order 1..6 — the centre of the level-n cell containing it, which is well defined
because the curve is self-similar. Each frame the drawn point is a chain of lerps up that
hierarchy, `Q_n = lerp(Q_{n-1}, pos_n, s_n)`, and each level's `s_n` is its own slow sine
(periods 55-135 s), so the levels fold in and out on independent clocks and the curve is
almost never at a pure integer order. Consecutive points are stroked as segments into a
640x480 float canvas with a 3-tap glow, hue runs along the arc length (210 palette steps end
to end) and a light pulse chases the path. The whole ribbon turns about a degree every twelve
seconds and breathes 5% in scale. 81-92% near-black — an overlay. 207 fps, mean delta 0.21.
