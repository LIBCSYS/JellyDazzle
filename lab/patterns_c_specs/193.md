# 193 — Cord Braid

Three plaited bands with genuine over-and-under. Each cord rides a cylinder —
`y = A sin(phase)`, `z = A cos(phase)` — with the four strands of a band evenly spaced in
phase and alternating in travel direction, which is exactly the geometry of a maypole
plait: half the cords run one way, half the other, and they must cross. The crossings are
not faked by draw order. Every cord sample stamps a depth-tested disc into a z-buffer at
480x360, so whichever cord is truly nearer wins the pixel, and each also lays a slightly
wider disc of the same hue at 6% brightness a little further back, which produces the dark
casing that makes knotwork readable at a glance. Shading is a quadratic dome across the
cord times a `0.52 + 0.48 z/A` depth term, so a cord dims as it dives behind its
neighbour. The three bands counter-scroll at 0.0026 to 0.0048 radians a frame over a long
sine warp that bows the whole band, and the cylinder radius breathes 10%, so the weave
never lines up the same way twice. Roughly 8000 samples a frame, 150 depth-tested pixels
each, then one glow pass. 174 fps, mean frame delta 0.81, 79-81% near-black — overlay,
and with three horizontal bands on black it leaves two wide dark stripes for a ground
underneath.
