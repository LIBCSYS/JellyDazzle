# 138 Soap Bubbles

**Look.** Sixteen bubbles drifting on black, each a dark transparent disc ringed by a band
of magenta-gold-cyan iridescence that crowds tighter and tighter toward the rim. A small
specular pip sits high-left on each. Where two bubbles overlap, their rims add and flare
white. Over minutes the colours walk upward through the interference orders — a ring that
was gold becomes magenta becomes cyan — the way a real film does as it drains. ~85%
near-black; the natural SCREEN overlay of this batch.

**Math.** No colour ramp is involved: the palette is produced by the optics. Each bubble is
shaded by three facts about a soap film. It drains under gravity, so thickness `T` runs
thin at the crown to thick at the foot (`0.34 + 0.66 v`). The viewing path through the film
grows as `1/sqrt(1 - u^2)` toward the rim, where `u` is normalised radius — clamped at 3.1
so the rim fringes stay resolvable instead of aliasing into noise. And the reflected
intensity in each channel is `sin^2(2 pi n T path / lambda)` evaluated at 650, 550 and
450 nm, read from a 1024-entry `sin^2` table with `n = 1.35` and `T` in micrometres. That
single expression yields the whole soap-bubble palette, the crowding of orders toward the
rim, and the black spot where the film thins below a quarter wave. Fresnel weighting `u^4`
keeps the centres transparent, so each bubble is a bright rim around a dark disc; the last
1.6 px are ramped for silhouette antialiasing. The palette enters only as a per-frame
modulation `0.42 + 0.58 c` on the three channels, which tints the iridescence without
flattening it. Paths use the golden angle for the vertical phase so the sixteen orbits do
not lock into bands. Measured 328 fps at 1280x960, delta 0.26-0.28.
