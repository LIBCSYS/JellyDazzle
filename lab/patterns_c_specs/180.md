# 180 — Hilbert Weave

A space-filling curve refining and coarsening itself. The Hilbert curves of order 4, 5 and
6 (256, 1024 and 4096 points) are generated once at init by the standard `d2xy` bit-twiddle
and normalised to the square. To move between two orders, the coarser path is resampled
uniformly in parameter onto the finer one's point count and the two are then linearly
interpolated: the thread does not jump from 1024 segments to 4096, it **grows** the extra
detail out of itself, budding new corners inside the existing ones and pulling them back
in again on the way down. The cycle is 4 -> 5 -> 6 -> 5 -> 4, smoothstepped, 760 frames a
leg, so the figure is continuously either sharpening or softening and never repeats a
still. A hue ramp runs along the curve's parameter and a brightness pulse chases it at
0.0075 rad/frame, which makes the direction of travel visible and turns an otherwise
static tiling into something with a current in it; the square turns at 0.00052 rad/frame
and breathes 4.5%. The half-size is set to 0.30*H so that the diagonal still clears the
frame at any rotation angle. One continuous glowing line, end to end, on black — this is
the most graphic, least atmospheric routine of the ten, and it composites like ink on
whatever is underneath. 80% near-black, mean frame delta 0.22.
