# 165 — Vortex Street

The von Karman wake behind a cylinder, drawn in dye rather than simulated on a grid.
Eighteen point vortices of alternating sign occupy the classic staggered double row at
+/-30 px from the wake axis, drifting downstream at 0.86 of the free-stream speed and
wrapping through a 470 px domain; each one's circulation is ramped up and down by a
smoothstep over the first 60 and last 90 px of its travel, so a wrapping vortex fades
in and out instead of popping. 7200 massless tracers are advected by the exact
Biot-Savart sum, `u = U + sum_k Gamma_k * (-dy, dx)/(dx^2 + dy^2 + 300)`, plus a
short-range radial push that makes dye go *around* the cylinder rather than through it;
tracers leaving the domain respawn at the inlet and their brightness is likewise ramped
over 55 px in and 70 px out. The picture is the streaklines: each tracer stamps into a
480x360 plate that decays 0.93/frame, so a particle's last ~15 frames of travel are
visible as a filament and the curls, pinches and hand-offs between vortices are drawn
by the flow itself rather than by any geometry. Dye above and below the wake axis is
tinted 60 ramp entries apart, which separates the two vortex rows by colour, and the
cylinder is outlined dimly so the cause of the wake is on screen. Nothing here is
scripted: change the vortex spacing and the wake reorganises. Measured: ~285 fps at
1280x960 (7200 tracers x 18 vortices = 130K interactions per frame), mean frame delta
1.44, 69% near-black — overlay-qualified, and the highest-motion pattern of this set,
still six times inside the budget.
